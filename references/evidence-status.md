# Capability and Evaluation Limits

- Style rules guide design and review; they do not guarantee consistent results across subjects. Landscape relationships do not establish transfer to people or animals.
- People and animals require additional checks for identity, anatomy, and spatial integration. See [failure cases](failure-cases.md).
- Record file validation, assistant visual review, and user approval separately. None substitutes for the others.
- Approval of one output does not establish general reliability or automatically make it a future reference.
- Generation is stochastic. Attempts without controlled variables cannot establish the causal effect of a prompt term.
- A few trials or tests on synthetic photographs cannot establish generalization to real photographs or exact identity preservation.
- Failure does not establish that a subject is permanently unsuitable. Editing the rules does not itself demonstrate improved generation quality.
