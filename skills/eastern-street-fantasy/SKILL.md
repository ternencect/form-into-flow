# Eastern Street Fantasy

Express character identity through a fusion of East Asian garment structures, streetwear components, and fantasy equipment. Emphasize contrasting volumes, bold color fields, and thematic graphics rather than a fixed accessory checklist. This is a working style definition for this project.

## Establish the style

- **Structural fusion:** Build an East Asian clothing vocabulary through broad sleeves, overlapping fronts, or layered draping, then integrate jacket volumes, zippers, webbing, technical fabrics, or sneakers into the same design. Choose sources that suit the character; do not treat clothing symbols from different cultures as interchangeable.
- **Proportional contrast:** Contrast one dominant volume with lighter or slimmer forms. Small heads, elongated limbs, and oversized garments are common tendencies. Mechanical bodies are an important option, but not every character needs to become a robot. Preserve the user's specified build, species, and bodily features.
- **Graphics and color:** Use a few dominant colors with a clear hierarchy of area and value. Reinforce the theme through large motifs, geometric divisions, or graffiti-like strokes. Favor vivid contrasts by default, while adapting hue and saturation to the character and the user's request.
- **Theme and density:** Let key equipment communicate occupation, abilities, or personality. Concentrate complexity in meaningful areas; keep other areas readable through larger shapes and less frequent detail. Garment surfaces do not need to be entirely blank.

Read the [design and review guide](references/style-guide.md) when developing a design further or diagnosing deviations.

## Follow the request

Determine whether the user wants a new character, a redesign, a written concept, or a critique. For text-only concepts or critiques, deliver that content without automatically generating images.

For a new character, start with one central motif and express it through silhouette, equipment, and graphics. Fill gaps with necessary design assumptions without automatically expanding the worldbuilding. For a redesign, inspect the input and identify the face, build, signature equipment, and user-specified features to preserve before deciding which garments, materials, and proportions can change.

When an image request has no composition requirements, default to one full-body character concept on a light neutral background, posed to make the design readable, with headwear and footwear fully in frame. Honor specified poses, settings, aspect ratios, and transparency. For a series, share proportional tendencies, graphics, and material language while giving each character a distinct silhouette and thematic equipment.

## Generate and edit

Use an available image-generation tool and follow the imagegen skill where provided. Deliver actual images when requested; explain the limitation if the tool is unavailable.

Describe concrete design relationships in the prompt: character theme and action, dominant silhouette, garment construction and equipment, primary and secondary color areas, graphic density, rendering and composition, and features to preserve or change. Use clear contours, hard-edged color fields, and selective material rendering. Labels such as “Eastern,” “streetwear,” and “fantasy” alone do not specify a design.

Inspect user-provided images and distinguish their roles: identity reference, style reference, or editing target. Identify the features to preserve or borrow. Save edits as new versions.

## Review and deliver

Inspect the actual result for a clear dominant silhouette, a readable theme, integrated East Asian and streetwear structures, a hierarchy of color and detail, and plausible connections between body parts and props. Consult the guide for observable signs. Address the most consequential design issue first rather than continually adding accessories or negative prompts.

Deliver the image with a brief design explanation, or the requested written concept or critique. State significant remaining deviations and distinguish assistant review from user approval.
